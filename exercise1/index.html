About Sergei Panteleevich Mavrodi

Sergei Panteleevich Mavrodi (11 August 1955 – 26 March 2018) was a Russian financial fraudster, financial criminal and previously a deputy of the State Duma. He was the founder of the МММ, a scheme that defrauded millions of people around the globe.

Mavrodi died of heart problems at the age of 62 at a hospital in Moscow

  1. During his childhood, he was diagnosed with a bilateral heart defect.
  2. He studied at the Moscow State Institute of Electronics and Mathematics. In 1983, Soviet police detained him for ten days due to illegal economic activity.
  3. In 1989 Mavrodi founded MMM. He was then imprisoned on charges of financial fraud involving MMM.
  4. In 1994 Mavrodi was elected deputy of State Duma obtaining parliamentary immunity, just three weeks after he was released from prison.
  5. In January 2011, Mavrodi launched another pyramid scheme called MMM-2011, asking investors to buy so-called Mavro currency units.
  6. In 2011 he launched a similar scheme in India, called MMM India, again stating clearly that the vehicle was a pyramid.
  7. In 1998 Mavrodi created Stock Generation, allegedly a classic pyramid scheme presented as a "virtual stock market game".
